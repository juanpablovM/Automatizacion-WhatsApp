# CRM 1.2d: contingency handoffs in the CRM
Objective: send bot handoffs to the CRM (`handoff.created` 1.2) with one owner each, let sellers reply without an opportunity (`conversation_id`), and close handoffs from the CRM (`handoff.acknowledged` / `handoff.resolved`).
Branch: `feat/crm-1-2d-handoffs` (local). Delivery strategy: ask-on-risk. Test runner: vitest (`npx vitest run --globals <file>`), DB tests with `TEST_PG_INTEGRATION=1` on the test Postgres (`docker-compose.test.yml`, `TEST_PGPORT`).

## Specs

- **S1 — Area routing (owner).** "si, reclamos a postventa y el resto a ventas". Complaint handoffs are `kind: complaint`, `area: claims`; every other bot handoff is `kind: commercial`, `area: sales`. A v3 handoff is a complaint when one of its reason observations has concept `issue_description`; legacy handoffs keep their current area (`claims` already for complaint/opt_out).
- **S2 — One owner per handoff (owner).** Sales without opportunity: "Assign by rotation (Recommended)". Post-sale staff: "Son las mimas vendedoras". Complaints: "Their seller, else rotation (Recommended)". The handoff carries `crm_opportunity_id` when the customer's phone has an opportunity (latest live lead with `crm_opportunity_id`); otherwise `crm_seller_id` of a seller chosen by the lead rotation, plus `phone` (E.164) and optional `name` (≤200 chars).
- **S3 — Re-escalation (owner).** "a la misma vendedora". A new handoff for a conversation whose previous handoff is still open reuses the open handoff's routing key (same `crm_opportunity_id` or same `crm_seller_id`), never the next seller.
- **S4 — `handoff.created` delivery (CRM contract, `.orca/drops/whatsapp-events-1.2d-dev-live.md`).** Protocol 1.2 only, exactly one routing key (both or neither is `400 invalid_event`). On `422 invalid_seller` the bot emits a new event (new `event_id`, same `handoff_id`) with the next seller in the rotation. Store `crm_case_id` when present. After the handoff is delivered, the conversation's messages and ownership changes go to the CRM exactly as after `lead.qualified` (earlier messages backfilled, then live).
- **S5 — `conversation_id` identity (CRM contract).** `chat.take`, `chat.release` and `message.send` carry exactly one of `crm_opportunity_id` or `conversation_id`; both or neither is `400 invalid_event`. Unknown `conversation_id` (no handoff delivered by the bot for it) is `422 unknown_conversation`. Without an opportunity only the handoff's routed seller (or a CRM admin) may act.
- **S6 — Closure events (CRM contract).** `handoff.acknowledged` / `handoff.resolved`, `data` exactly `{handoff_id, crm_seller_id}`. Transitions as ClickUp closure: `notified→acknowledged`, `notified|acknowledged→resolved`; resolved closes the conversation when it is `escalation_required` and also resolves every other open handoff of that conversation (re-escalations). Responses: `200 {"event_id","result":"applied"|"duplicate"}`, `409 invalid_transition`, `422 unknown_handoff`, `503` retry.
- **S8 — Reply without a lead (owner, 2026-11-25).** Answer to G1: "Fix before deploying". A conversation handed off to the CRM whose customer has no lead can be taken, released and replied from the CRM with the same effects as a lead-owned chat (bot silent while the seller holds it, 2 h window, release restores the bot), without creating a lead or a CRM opportunity.
- **S9 — Opportunity-keyed chat events silence the bot on a returning customer (owner, 2026-11-25: "si, me parece").** When `chat.take` / `chat.release` / `message.send` arrive by `crm_opportunity_id` and the customer has a newer lead than the opportunity's lead, the events act on the lease the bot reads for silence (the contact's latest lead, `contact_latest_lead_id`), so the bot goes silent in the conversation where the customer is writing; another seller's live lease there answers `409 ownership_conflict`. When the opportunity's lead is the latest lead, behavior is unchanged.
- **S10 — `conversation_id` accepted on any handed-off conversation (owner, 2026-11-26: "Yes, accept conversation_id (Recommended)").** `chat.take` / `chat.release` / `message.send` by `conversation_id` are accepted for every conversation with a delivered `handoff.created`, also when that handoff was routed by `crm_opportunity_id` or the customer has an opportunity. They act on the customer's current chat lease (the contact's latest lead when the customer has a lead, as in 049 and T6/B2; the conversation key only when the customer has no lead). Unknown or never-handed-off conversations still answer `422 unknown_conversation`.
- **S11 — CRM admins may act (owner, 2026-11-26: "CRM admin flag (Recommended)").** `chat.take` / `chat.release` / `message.send` accept a CRM admin as `crm_seller_id` whether or not it is active in the rotation: new `sellers.crm_is_admin`, set by an operator, never read by the lead rotation and excluded from the handoff rotation (sellers 6 «Juan Pablo» and 9 «Jesus, CRM dev admin» in production). An inactive seller that is not an admin is still `422 invalid_seller`; holder and conflict rules are unchanged (an admin gets 409 while another seller holds the chat).
- **S7 — Rollout.** Behind a flag, off by default; no change to ClickUp workflows/SQL behavior. Production deploy only after the owner approves; then the CRM's 5-step test with the test phone.
