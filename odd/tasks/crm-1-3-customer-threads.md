# CRM 1.3: customer threads (Conversations page keyed by customer)
Objective: give the CRM every WhatsApp customer as one thread (`contact_id`), with the bot's state per customer for classification, chat actions by customer, and a one-time historical backfill. Contract: `.orca/drops/whatsapp-events-1.3-*.md` (proposal, crm-response, bot-reply, crm-closed-history, bot-version-zero).
Branch: `feat/crm-1-3-customer-threads`. Delivery strategy: ask-on-risk. Test runner: vitest (`npx vitest run --globals <file>`), DB tests `TEST_PG_INTEGRATION=1` on the test Postgres.

## Specs

- **S1 — Owner idea (2026-11-25).** The Conversations page shows "every WhatsApp customer", "classified by the bot's state" and "prioritized: customers ready to quote and those furthest along in the bot's process first". Owner (2026-11-26): "en el CRM solamente estoy pidiendo que se muestren todas las conversaciones y el historial de whatsapp, para que netamente tengan visión de todo y que sea el mismo servicio que esta ofreciendo whatsapp, whatsapp web. Y para el momento de que una conversación que exista o no en el historial, sea categorizada, y puesta en alguna prioridad y/o categoria".
- **S2 — Envelope 1.3 (CRM response §1).** New `protocol_revision: "1.3"`; `contact_id` = `wa-<prefix>-contact-<id>`, one per customer and WhatsApp line, stable forever, required on every 1.3 event. `conversation_id` required on `message.new`, `lead.qualified`, `handoff.created`, `ownership.taken`, `ownership.released`; absent on `contact.upserted` and `contact.state`. A `conversation_id` always belongs to the same `contact_id` (else `422 contact_mismatch`). Events with 1.1/1.2 keep their rules.
- **S3 — `contact.upserted` (§2).** `data: {revision, phone (E.164), name (≤200 or null)}`; growing `revision` per contact; responses `applied | ignored_stale | duplicate`, 409 on changed bytes. Sent first for a new customer.
- **S4 — `contact.state` (§3).** `data: {state_version, state, progress, stage, conversation_id, changed_at}`; `state` ∈ `ready_to_quote, handed_off, seller_held, qualifying, idle`; `progress` 0–100 (answered required questions / required questions); `stage` = the bot's current question id (list sent in bot-reply §1) or null; `conversation_id` null for `idle`; `state_version` is the only ordering rule. Independent of `message.new` (no state needed just because the customer wrote). `handed_off` only with an open handoff the CRM already has. Ownership events stay the authority for silence.
- **S5 — Every customer reaches the CRM.** `message.new` for every conversation under its `contact_id`, not only announced ones (proposal §2; CRM agreed volume).
- **S6 — Chat actions by customer (§4.1–4.2).** `chat.take` / `chat.release` / `message.send` carry exactly one of `crm_opportunity_id`, `conversation_id`, `contact_id` (zero or two → 400). Any active CRM seller (`ventas`) or CRM admin (`crm_is_admin`) can take any customer's chat at any time, including `qualifying` (owner decision 1: "A seller can take any customer's chat at any time"); holder rules unchanged (409).
- **S7 — `contact.opportunity_linked` (CRM → bot, §4.3; accepted).** `data: {contact_id, crm_opportunity_id, crm_seller_id}`; after 200 later handoffs of that customer carry that `crm_opportunity_id`, its seller is the holder, a later `lead.qualified` carries the same id. `200 applied|duplicate`, `409 identity_conflict` (customer linked to another open opportunity), `422` unknown contact / invalid seller.
- **S8 — Per-contact ownership counter (§4.4; accepted).** `ownership.taken` / `ownership.released` and command responses carry one counter per `contact_id`, started above the highest per-conversation version ever sent for any of its conversations.
- **S9 — Backfill (§5 + closed history).** Once, against the CRM production receiver when 1.3 is switched on there (not the laptop dev receiver). Per contact: `contact.upserted`; its `lead.qualified` and `handoff.created` oldest first (an `open` lead's `lead.qualified` before its handoffs); every `message.new` in `sent_at` order with `"historical": true`; then the current `contact.state`. Owner (2026-11-26, confirmed "dale"): only open leads become opportunities — `lead.qualified` `"historical": true` with `outcome` `won|lost|open` and `closed_at` (required for won/lost); won/lost answer `status: historical` and are never used for routing. `handoff.created` `"historical": true` with optional `resolved_at` (resolved → stored resolved, no notification; without it → live handoff, 1.2d rules). Paced batches, retry 503.
- **S11 — Outcome rule for the backfill (owner, 2026-11-26: "Age cutoff (Recommended)").** `concretada` = won; `no concretada` and `cerrada` = lost (historical, no opportunity); a lead with no ClickUp status is `open` (an opportunity) only if it is 30 days old or less, older ones are lost. Test-phone leads (`LEAD_TEST_PHONE_NUMBERS`) are never backfilled. `closed_at` = the time that status was reached (`clickup_status_at`), or the lead's last activity for age-cutoff losses.
- **S10 — Rollout.** Behind a flag, off by default, and the phone allowlist; ClickUp paths untouched; production switch only after the CRM confirms its receiver accepts 1.3 and the owner approves.
